## 🚀 Key Features & Services (Mukammal Tafseelat)

### 1. 📱 Rozana Ki Delivery Entry (Super Fast Stepper)
- Driver ya delivery boy mobile se hi customer ko select kar k **+ / -** daba kar bottle add kar sakta hai.
- **On-the-spot Cash Entry:** Agar customer ne usi waqt delivery k waqt paise diye hain, to usi screen se payment bhi add ho jati hai.
- Na register ki zaroorat, na kisi parchi ki!

---

### 2. 🧾 Automatic Monthly Bill & ERP Customer Ledger
- Har customer ka date-wise mukammal record:
  - **Opening Balance** (Pichla baqaya ya customer ka advance).
  - **Rozana ki Delivery** (Tareekh, Bottle count, Rate, Total charges).
  - **Payment Received** (Cash, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Bank).
  - **Running Balance** (Har delivery aur payment k baad ka live baqaya).
- **1-Click WhatsApp Share:** Aik button dabate hi customer k WhatsApp pr mukammal bill message chala jata hai.
- **Downloadable PDF Invoice:** Plant k logo, phone number, address aur stamps k sath professional PDF print ya share karein.

---

### 3. 🧴 Bottle Security Deposit & Inventory Tracker
- Water plant ka sab se bara sarmaya uski **19-Liter Pet/Polycarbonate Bottles** hoti hain.
- Har customer se li gayi security amanat (Cash Deposit) aur unk pas mojud total bottles ka live track.
- Koi customer connection khatam kare to 1 click me refund status aur bottle return record ho jata hai.
- Ek bhi bottle zaya ya chori nahi hogi!

---

### 4. 💰 Udhaar & Payment Recovery (Khata System)
- Ek nazar me pata chal jata hai kis customer pr kitna udhaar (Due Balance) baqi hai aur kin customers ka Advance paisa plant k pas jama hai.
- Payments filter karein: Cash, Online transfer, EasyPaisa, JazzCash.
- Recovery boy ko bhejte waqt list mobile pr open karein aur recovery asan banayein.

---

### 5. 📊 Wholesale Cost, Plant Expenses & Net Profit Ledger
- Sirf sale dekhna kaafi nahi hota, asal cheez **Safai aur Munafa (Profit)** hai:
  - Wholesale plant cost per bottle.
  - Daily Plant Expenses (Bijli ka bill, RO plant maintenance, gari ka diesel, driver ki tankhwah, bottle caps/labels).
  - **Automatic Net Profit Calculation:** System khud batata hai k is mahine aapka asal munafa kitna hua!

---

### 6. 📱 100% Mobile Friendly (Kahin Se Bhi Use Karein)
- Kisi mehnge computer ya system ki zaroorat nahi.
- Aap, aapka manager ya delivery boy apne **Android ya iPhone** se asani se chala sakta hai.
- Clean, simple aur tez raftaar interface jo har umar ka shakhs 5 minute me seekh sakta hai.

---

### 7. ☁️ Cloud Backup & 100% Data Safety
- Register phat jane, pani girne ya chori hone ka koi khatra nahi.
- Data secure cloud servers par mehfooz rehta hai. Saal purana hisab bhi 2 second me mil jata hai.

## 🛠️ Additional Custom Features (Jo Client Ki Demand Pr Add Ho Sakte Hain)

Aapke business ki specific requirements k mutabiq hum mazeed modern features bhi shamil kar sakte hain:

1. **📲 Automatic SMS Alerts:**
   - Bottle deliver hote hi ya payment receive hote hi customer ko automatic SMS alert chala jaye.
2. **🚚 Route / Area Wise Delivery Boy Login:**
   - Har delivery boy ka apna login ho, wo sirf apne route k customers dekh sake aur unki delivery mark kare.
3. **📍 GPS & Route Optimization (Google Maps):**
   - Delivery van k liye short-cut route aur customer location pin taake diesel aur waqt bache.
4. **🏷️ QR Code / Barcode Scanning:**
   - Har 19L bottle pr unique QR code/barcode stick hoga, driver scan kar k bottle issue aur return karega.
5. **🏢 Multi-Branch / Multiple Plants Support:**
   - Agar aapke 1 se zyada plants ya delivery depots hain, to ek hi master dashboard se tamam branches ka hisab.
6. **🔔 Payment Reminder Automation:**
   - Mahine ki makhsoos tareekh (jaise 1st ya 5th) ko jin customers ka udhaar baqi ho, unhe automated reminder notification chali jaye.
7. **🧪 Water Quality & Filter Change Reminder:**
   - RO Plant membrane, sand filter, carbon filter aur UV lamp change ki expiry alert aur TDS log.
