KIYA AAP AB BHI PURANE REGISTER, KACHE PARCHON AUR UDHAAR ME ULJHE HUE HAIN?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Har mahine bottle ki chori, register khona, udhaar ka bhool jana aur delivery
boy ka hisab na milna aapke karobar ko lakhon rupay ka nuqsan punchata hai.
Ye software aapke business ka munafa barhane aur sar dard khatam karne k liye
design kiya gya hai!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IS SYSTEM SY AAPKA KAAM KAISE ASAN HOGA? (Pehle vs Ab)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[PEHLE - Purana Tareeqa / Register]
* Rozana gari wapsi pr register pr bottle likhna aur kanta-piti karna.
* Mahine k aakhir me har customer ka hisab jodna aur bill banana (3-4 din zaya).
* Customer kehta hai "Mene to itni bottle nahi li" ya "Pichla hisab ghalat hai".
* Empty bottle ka hisab na hona (Botal market me zaya ya chori ho jana).
* Mahine k aakhir me pata hi nahi chalta ke sach me NET PROFIT kitna hua.

[AB - Is Software K Baad]
* Mobile pr 1 tap me bottle delivery aur received cash ka entry.
* Automatic 1-Click PDF Bill aur WhatsApp par seedha message.
* Complete ERP Ledger (Kis tareekh ko kitni bottle li, kitna payment diya).
* Bottle Security & Return Tracker - Har customer k pas kitni botlein hain.
* Wholesale Cost, Plant Expenses (Bijli, Diesel, Labor) nikaal kar exact Munafa.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY FEATURES & SERVICES (Mukammal Tafseelat)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. ROZANA KI DELIVERY ENTRY (Super Fast Mobile Stepper)
   - Driver ya delivery boy mobile se customer select kar k (+ / -) se bottle add
     kar sakta hai.
   - On-the-spot Cash Entry: Agar customer ne delivery k waqt paise diye hain,
     to usi screen se payment bhi add ho jati hai.
   - Na register ki zaroorat, na kisi kachi parchi ki!

2. AUTOMATIC MONTHLY BILL & ERP CUSTOMER LEDGER
   - Har customer ka date-wise mukammal ledger:
     * Opening Balance (Pichla baqaya ya customer ka advance).
     * Rozana ki Delivery (Tareekh, Bottle count, Rate, Charges).
     * Payment Received (Cash, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Bank).
     * Running Balance (Har entry k baad ka live baqaya).
   - 1-Click WhatsApp Share: 1 click me customer k WhatsApp pr bill send karein.
   - Downloadable PDF Invoice: Plant k logo, phone number, address aur stamps k
     sath professional PDF invoice print ya share karein.

3. BOTTLE SECURITY DEPOSIT & INVENTORY TRACKER
   - Water plant ka sab se bara sarmaya uski 19-Liter Bottles hoti hain.
   - Har customer se li gayi security amanat (Cash Deposit) aur unk pas mojud
     total bottles ka live record.
   - Koi customer connection khatam kare to 1 click me refund status aur bottle
     return record ho jata hai.
   - Ek bhi bottle zaya ya chori nahi hogi!

4. UDHAAR & PAYMENT RECOVERY (Khata System)
   - Ek nazar me pata chal jata hai kis customer pr kitna udhaar (Due Balance)
     baqi hai aur kin customers ka Advance paisa plant k pas jama hai.
   - Payments filter karein: Cash, Online transfer, EasyPaisa, JazzCash.
   - Recovery boy ko bhejte waqt list mobile pr open karein aur recovery asan banayein.

5. WHOLESALE COST, PLANT EXPENSES & NET PROFIT LEDGER
   - Sirf sale dekhna kaafi nahi hota, asal cheez Safai aur Munafa (Profit) hai:
     * Wholesale plant cost per bottle.
     * Daily Plant Expenses (Bijli ka bill, RO plant maintenance, gari ka diesel,
       driver ki tankhwah, bottle caps/labels).
     * Automatic Net Profit Calculation: System khud batata hai k is mahine aapka
       asal munafa kitna hua!

6. 100% MOBILE FRIENDLY (Kahin Se Bhi Use Karein)
   - Kisi mehnge computer ya system ki zaroorat nahi.
   - Aap, aapka manager ya delivery boy apne Android ya iPhone se chala sakta hai.
   - Clean, simple aur tez raftaar interface jo har umar ka shakhs 5 minute me
     seekh sakta hai.

7. CLOUD BACKUP & 100% DATA SAFETY
   - Register phat jane, pani girne ya chori hone ka koi khatra nahi.
   - Data secure cloud servers par mehfooz rehta hai. Saal purana hisab bhi
     2 second me mil jata hai.

ADDITIONAL CUSTOM FEATURES (Jo Client Ki Demand Pr Add Ho Sakte Hain)

Aapke business ki specific requirements k mutabiq hum mazeed features bhi add
kar sakte hain:

   Bottle deliver hote hi ya payment receive hote hi customer ko automatic SMS.

   Har delivery boy ka apna login ho, wo sirf apne route k customers dekh sake.

3. GPS & Route Optimization (Google Maps):
   Delivery van k liye short-cut route aur customer location pin taake diesel
   aur waqt bache.

4. QR Code / Barcode Scanning:
   Har 19L bottle pr unique QR code/barcode stick hoga, driver scan kar k bottle
   issue aur return karega.

5. Multi-Branch / Multiple Plants Support:
   Agar aapke 1 se zyada plants ya depots hain, to ek hi master dashboard se
   tamam branches ka hisab.

6. Automated Payment Reminder:
   Mahine ki makhsoos tareekh (jaise 1st ya 5th) ko udhaar waly customers ko
   auto reminder notification chali jaye.

7. Water Quality & Filter Change Reminder:
   RO Plant membrane, sand filter, carbon filter aur UV lamp change ki expiry
   alert aur TDS log.
